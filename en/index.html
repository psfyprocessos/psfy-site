Aerial work platform rental, operating in four Brazilian states

Purchasing for 9 branches in a single process

When the purchasing process started to stall, five branches were buying at the same time, each one its own way, by email and WhatsApp groups. Requests got lost in the middle of conversations and nobody knew who had approved what.

The decision that shaped the project

One process for every branch, not one per branch. Each branch still sees its own requests and management now sees everything.

  • 6,016orders completed in 18 months
  • 9branches in the same process, up from 5 at the start
  • 12 daysmedian lead time, from order to completion
  • 85.4%of orders completed within 30 days

What is inside the workflow

  • A standard form that asks for the right information up front
  • A defined owner for each stage
  • Recorded approval, showing who approved it and when
  • Invoice issued automatically

Only 4.2% of orders were canceled, a sign that requests arrive right from the start.

The sign that it worked

The workflow was built only for buying items. Over time it took in the company's other purchases, which used to be scattered across email and WhatsApp. Today, item purchases are less than a third of everything that goes through it.

Version 2 in progress. Items and services in separate workflows, approval limits by amount, orders from the same supplier grouped into a single order, and time metrics for each stage.

The billing error was not in the calculation. It was in the retyping.

Labhoro had errors in its billing. Before proposing any tool, we mapped the entire operation to find the cause, with nine interviews, from the board to the brokers.

What we proposed

Questions that always come up

Do I need to be using Pipefy already?

No. If it makes sense for your process, we build it from scratch. You get the license directly at pipefy.com, PSFY does not resell it.

What if my process does not fit in Pipefy?

We use the tool that solves it. Make, Zapier, Typebot and AI come in when the process calls for them, always with the simplest recommendation that works.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the process and the format. Your first engagement is the Hours Package, with 10 hours and training included, and before each stage you get an hours estimate. To find out the price for your case, request a quote and you will hear back the same day.

Do you work with companies outside Brazil?

Yes. All the work is remote, with online meetings, and we already serve operations in several Brazilian states and a client in the United States.

Is my data safe?

Yes. PSFY follows an internal data security protocol on every project and only recommends tools compliant with LGPD, Brazil's General Data Protection Law. The details are on the security and compliance page.

How does payment work?

Bank transfer, invoice, PIX in Brazil, or credit card through PayPal with a 4.99% surcharge, which is PayPal's fee.
